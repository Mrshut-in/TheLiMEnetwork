LiME Community Rules

Welcome to LiME! These rules keep our community fun, fair, and welcoming for everyone.

General Community Rules

These rules apply everywhere in the LiME Network — Discord, Minecraft Server, website, and all related spaces.

  1. Be Respectful to Everyone
    Treat all members with kindness. Harassment, hate speech, discrimination, bullying, or toxicity of any kind is not allowed.
  2. Keep Content Family-Friendly (SFW)
    No sexual, gory, violent, explicit, or otherwise inappropriate content or builds.
  3. No Advertising
    Advertising other servers, realms, social media, products, or services is not allowed without staff approval.
  4. No Drama or Personal Conflicts
    Keep personal arguments out of public chats and the server. Take issues to DMs or contact staff privately.
  5. No Political or Religious Debates
    These topics can cause unnecessary conflict and are not permitted in public areas.
  6. Respect Staff Decisions
    All staff instructions and decisions are final. Do not argue with or publicly criticize staff actions. (Except maybe Pricedbear)
  7. No Evading Punishments
    Using alternate accounts, VPNs, name changes, or any other method to bypass mutes, bans, or punishments is strictly prohibited.
  8. Protect Privacy
    Do not share or leak anyone’s personal information, private messages, screenshots, or sensitive details without permission.
  9. Respect Personal Boundaries
    No creepy, harassing, or unwanted messages to other members — in public or DMs.

Minecraft Server Rules (Java Edition – Bedrock Compatible)

These rules are specific to the LiME in-game server in Minecraft.

  1. No Cheating or Unauthorized Mods
    Hacked clients, unfair advantage tools, or unapproved mods are not allowed. Performance-enhancing mods are okay if approved by staff.
  2. No Offensive Builds
    Builds, signs, symbols, or map art that are offensive, sexual, political, or discriminatory are not allowed.
  3. No Exploits or Glitches
    Report bugs to staff. Using glitches, exploits, or unintended mechanics for unfair advantage is forbidden.
  4. PvP Rules
    Player-versus-player combat is only allowed in designated areas or with mutual consent. Spawn-killing and harassment PvP are not allowed.
  5. No Lag Machines or Excessive Farms
    Large redstone contraptions, AFK farms, or anything that causes lag must be staff-approved.
  6. Respect the server atmosphere
    Avoid disruptive or out-of-theme behavior that breaks immersion.

Important Notes

  • All punishments are at staff discretion and can vary based on severity and player history.
  • Staff decisions are final.
  • If you have questions or concerns, contact a staff member politely.

By joining LiME Network (our minecraft server) you agree to follow these rules. Thank you for helping keep our community awesome! □
