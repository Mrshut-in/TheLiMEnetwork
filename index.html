Key Features

  • True Crossplay — Java, Bedrock in the same world
  • Full VR Support — Native Vivecraft (including QuestCraft)
  • Player-Built Cities — Neighborhoods, apartments, shops, skyscrapers
  • Real Economy & Jobs — Work, earn money, run shops, buy land
  • Smart Phone Menu — Teleport, homes, warps, jobs, mail — right from your hotbar
  • Quality of Life — TreeCapitator, VeinMiner (crouch to chop whole trees, and ore veins), use crafting tables/anvils straight from inventory
  • Proximity Voice Chat — Talk to people next to you (Bedrock gets speech-to-text, this is still in beta)
  • Chill & Active Staff — No drama, no P2W, frequent events & updates
  • Always in the latest update! Right now 26.2, soon to be 26.3 (You can join in any mc version starting at 1.8.0)
