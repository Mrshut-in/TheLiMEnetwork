WHO I AM

Hey everyone! I'm Kainyx, Owner of LiME Network. Ever since I found Minecraft I wanted to make something that people would play. When I found the server I found what I wanted to do — so now we're here saving and helping LiME grow.

Outside of games I enjoy [Legos, TikTok lives, hiking, music, etc.]. From a person who didn't know how to code anything to someone who knows a bit more thanks to Optic — a true friend. This server is our project together.

WHAT I DO AS OWNER

As Owner my main job is making LiME a safe and respectful place and making sure nothing breaks:

  • ✅ Building & maintaining the spawn, hubs, and community areas
  • ✅ Working with the team to create new events, minigames & seasons
  • ✅ Keeping the server fair, friendly, and drama-free
  • ✅ Listening to player feedback and turning ideas into real features
  • ✅ Making sure Java, Bedrock & VR players all feel at home

I also help run the Discord, design custom maps, and make sure we always follow Minecraft's official guidelines.

WHY IS LiME IMPORTANT TO ME

LiME wasn't just another project for me — it was a piece of my childhood that I couldn't let go. It's also a safe space where I can vent or chill after a long day.

LiME isn't just a community… it's a family. We support each other no matter who you are.
